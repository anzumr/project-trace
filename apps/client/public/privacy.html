Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

krazer.io is a free browser game run by an independent developer. This page explains what information the game collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: anzum2225@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you create an account: your email address, your chosen username and the date you joined. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.
  • Google sign-in. If you choose “Sign in with Google”, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture link with us. We use your email to identify your account and your name only to suggest a username.
  • Gameplay. Results of online matches you finish: wins, losses, rounds, damage, accuracy and similar statistics, XP and level. Your username is shown to opponents. Signed-in players can see usernames and aggregate gameplay statistics on global leaderboards. Friends leaderboards compare you with your accepted friends.
  • Friends and invitations. We store friend requests, accepted friendships and private-duel invitations, including the room code and expiration time. Requests and invitations are visible to the players involved. You can decline or cancel requests, remove friends and dismiss invitations.
  • Technical information. Your IP address and connection details are processed by the game server and our hosting provider to deliver the game, prevent abuse and rate-limit requests.
  • Stored in your browser. Your settings (crosshair, sensitivity, audio, graphics) and your sign-in session are kept in your browser’s local storage. Offline practice stays in your browser and is never uploaded.

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not sell your information or use it for ads.

How we use it

  • To run your account, matchmaking and private rooms.
  • To save your progress, statistics and match history.
  • To connect friends, deliver duel invitations and show leaderboards.
  • To send account emails, such as email confirmation and password resets.
  • To keep the game secure and fair.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase: accounts, sign-in and the database that stores your progress.
  • Render: hosting for the website and game server.
  • Google: only if you use Google sign-in.
  • Our email delivery provider: sending account emails.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep account and gameplay data while your account exists. To delete your account and its data, sign in and choose Account → Delete account. This removes your account, username, progress, rank, statistics and friends right away. Matches you played stay in your opponents’ history, with your name replaced by “Deleted player”, and your username becomes available to other players. You can also email anzum2225@gmail.com from the address on the account and we will remove it. You can clear browser-stored settings at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Children

krazer.io is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
